How Remote Planning Works

All planning support is provided remotely. Aureliya does not attend events onsite. Onsite staffing and event-day services require separately agreed local coverage.

We agree the planning scope and deliverables before work begins. You choose and contract with venues, vendors, and any local coordinator, and pay them directly.
